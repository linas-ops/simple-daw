Press record and play your kit

Your module keeps sounding the way it always does while you play. Simple DAW captures every hit, then plays it back with a realistic kit.

  1. 1Plug your drum module into the computer with USB.
  2. 2Hit any pad. Its name lights up on the left.
  3. 3Press R or the red button, wait for the count-in, and play.
Velocityhow hard each hit is
Tip: press R to record, Space to play.
